ABOUT THE PLATFORM

ContextLens 3.0

A repository intelligence workspace built around UnvibeCode exports. Analyze a public codebase, inspect its generated relationships, audit context selections, and document source-backed findings.

ContextLens does not replace source-code review. Generated insights can be incomplete or incorrect; engineering judgment remains essential.
